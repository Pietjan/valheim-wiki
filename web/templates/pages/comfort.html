Comfort and rest

Comfort is counted once per kind of furniture: two chairs are no better than one, but a chair, a table and a rug add up. For each biome, the most comfort a base can reach with what can be built by then, the pieces that give it, and how long the rested bonus then lasts: eight minutes, and a minute more for every level of comfort.
