About

Where this comes from

Every number on this site is read out of the game's own files: the Unity asset bundles the Valheim client ships, version {{.Site.DB.Meta.Game}}, read on {{.Site.DB.Meta.Extracted}}. Nothing is typed in by hand except the English names of places the game never names, and the words around the numbers.

How

  • Items, recipes, pieces, creatures are the components on the game's prefabs: ItemDrop, Recipe, Piece, Character, CharacterDrop and the rest, with the defaults the game keeps for fields it does not use stripped by item type.
  • Spawning is the four spawn lists the server runs per zone, the raid table, and the spawners standing inside each location's prefab.
  • Locations are the world generator's placement rules, and what stands inside each prefab: chests with their loot tables, spawners, pickables, runestones and altars.
  • Your world is a port of the generator itself, run in your browser: the biomes, the rivers, and every rule's placement attempt in the order the game makes them. It reproduces real worlds to the metre; what it cannot know is anything decided after the seed, such as which way a randomly turned ruin faces.
  • Icons are the game's sprites; the round location and creature icons are rendered from the game's models.

Your seed

The seed you enter stays in your browser's storage along with the world it computed. Nothing is sent anywhere; there is no server.
