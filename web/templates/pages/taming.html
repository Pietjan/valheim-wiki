Taming and breeding

A wild animal is tamed by being fed, out of sight, until the timer runs down; it eats only what is listed, and the taming mead halves the wait. Tame pairs breed when fed and close, up to a crowd; the young grow in the time given. Chickens hatch from an egg kept warm by a fire under a roof.
