Tool

Planner

List what you mean to build and make: the pieces of a new settlement, the gear for the next biome, a stack of arrows. The planner adds up what it all costs, follows every ingredient back to the ore and the wood it starts as, works out the stations and the levels it takes, and sorts the materials by what a portal will carry. Keep as many plans as you like, and split one into stages to plan a whole playthrough: what an earlier stage builds is counted as standing in the later ones. Plans stay in this browser; a link hands one to the crew.

          Stations

            Costs are the recipes' and the pieces' as the game ships them (game {{.Site.DB.Meta.Game}}). Upgrades cost the per-level amount once for the second quality and twice for the third, then four times for the fourth and half as much again for each beyond, as the client's code counts it. A material is counted at its first recipe or conversion; a thing that can be smelted from more than one ore shows the choice. Fuel is counted for smelters at their rate per bar; a cooking fire's wood is not. Portals refuse what the game marks as not teleportable, unless your world's modifiers let everything through.
